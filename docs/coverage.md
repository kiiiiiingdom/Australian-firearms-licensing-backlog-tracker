**Archive snapshot: 2026-10-20 / 资料库快照：2026-10-20**. This matrix summarizes *verified records in this repository*, not everything published by governments. No archive entry does **not** mean a government has published nothing. / 矩阵仅说明本仓库已收录、已核实的记录；未收录不代表政府未公开。

## Read the matrix / 如何阅读

- **Within 30 d / 近期** and **31–90 d / 较旧** measure days from the original official source date to the archive snapshot. They do **not** certify a currently active policy or a currently valid queue estimate.
- **>90 d / 历史** denotes older official source dates; a historical count or staffing figure must not be interpreted as the current total.
- **Future effective / 未来日期** is a separately flagged future-dated record, not a current statistic. **Undated / 未注明** cannot be age-classified.
- **Not in archive / 未收录** means no matching verified record for this narrowly defined metric, never zero delay or confirmation that the authority publishes nothing.
- **Queue-date** means published processing date lag. Official means, backlog counts, staffing figures and policies are *different metric families*. / 队列日期、平均办理耗时、积压数量和人员口径不能混算。
