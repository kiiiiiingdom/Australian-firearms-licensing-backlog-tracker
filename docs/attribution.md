### Reuse of government-sourced figures / 官方数据与衍生图表

Every chart represents **our own calculations and presentation** from the identified source records. A public numeric fact is not the same as permission to copy a source's whole page, written report, images, marks or tables.

- **South Australia Police:** for calculations from SAPOL content, give the source attribution **Based on: South Australia Police material**, and link the specific official page, with dates. See [SAPOL's copyright and derivative attribution rules](https://www.police.sa.gov.au/copyright). / 从 SAPOL 的公开日期计算衍生积压天数时使用其要求的衍生作品署名，并提供官方日期。
- **NSW Police Force:** cite the NSW Police Force, give its requested **© State of New South Wales (NSW Police Force)** attribution, and link the original page. Check its [copyright exclusions](https://www.police.nsw.gov.au/services/additional_services/freedom_of_information/more_information?a=79).
- **Queensland Police Service:** cite the [QPS original source](https://www.police.qld.gov.au/copyright) and note that its self-owned site material generally uses CC BY 4.0 subject to trademark/third-party exceptions.
- **NSW and WA Parliaments:** cite each parliament and original record. Their [NSW](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/copyright) and [WA](https://www.parliament.wa.gov.au/WebCMS/WebCMS.nsf/content/copyright) terms provide conditional permission for some materials, **not** for third-party committee submissions.
- **NT Police and ACT Policing:** link to the original source and give an independently written factual account rather than reproducing original site text without checking specific permission. See [NT terms](https://pfes.nt.gov.au/node/849) and [ACT terms](https://police.act.gov.au/disclaimer).

**No logos, insignia, photographs, original PDFs or whole official pages are required for the website's charts.** The CC BY 4.0 notice for the maintainers' *original* compilation does not relicense third-party documents. No site-level notice should be treated as permission for every attached publication.
