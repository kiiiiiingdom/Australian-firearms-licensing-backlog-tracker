# Contribute official data / 参与贡献

This site is built from official public administrative evidence. Contributions are reviewed before being merged. The underlying repository remains private during its pre-publication review; community participation will be opened only after explicit publication approval.

此网站基于官方公开行政证据，所有数据须经审核合并。仓库目前仍处于公开前审计阶段；正式邀请外部贡献将在得到明确发布批准后进行。

**Guides:**
- [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/kiiiiiingdom/Australian-firearms-licensing-backlog-tracker/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [AI evidence research prompt](https://github.com/kiiiiiingdom/Australian-firearms-licensing-backlog-tracker/blob/main/prompts/official-source-contributor.md)
- [CSV header](https://github.com/kiiiiiingdom/Australian-firearms-licensing-backlog-tracker/blob/main/templates/observations-header.csv)

**Rules:** Official original sources; separate metric/report dates; preserve exact short quotations; no overwriting historic evidence; no private applicant details; do not fabricate a metric or an official source.

必须使用官方原始来源，保存核验原文及日期，不覆盖历史证据，不记录私人申请信息，也不能编造指标。
