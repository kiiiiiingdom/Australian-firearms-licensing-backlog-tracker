## Accuracy and privacy / 准确性及隐私

Published observations have heterogeneous statistical definitions and source dates. Relative indices are descriptive, not a prediction of individual waiting times or staff productivity. The dataset excludes private applicant records.

Official-source excerpts are kept short and linked to their provenance. If an original publisher's permission or required attribution is unclear, review that source before further republication.

本数据不提供法律意见、申请进度推算或员工生产率排行。原始官方摘录保持简短并注明来源；第三方材料再发布前须单独核查授权。
