Official method/caveat wording:
- Page 3 (zero-indexed 2): "Gun Safe data does not include paper-based applications. Paper based applications represent 2.5% of all transactions."
- Page 3: "Average processing times for paper-based applications are longer than online applications due to delays associated with postage and manual handling."
- Average-time chart caveat: "Average Processing Times is inclusive of 28 day cooling off period" for PTA; new-licence chart says average times are inclusive of the 28-day cooling-off period.
- PTA finalisation chart includes outcomes/states including expired, issued, lapsed, refused, re-issued, revoked, suspended, used and finalised; this count is not equivalent to applications newly approved.

Methodological handling:
- Keep 3 August monthly average-time observations separate from 6 monthly application/processing counts.
- No processing-date queue backlog-age or speed-ratio calculations apply.
- Counts of applications submitted and processed/finalised during the same month are different date cohorts and sometimes differently scoped. Their difference must not be reported as an independently verified backlog count.
- August vs July 2026 changes, same official series: new licence 47.76 -> 45.22 days (-2.54); PTA 101.26 -> 104.17 days (+2.91); renewal 15.88 -> 40.76 days (+24.88).
