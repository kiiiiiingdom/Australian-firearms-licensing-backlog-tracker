Interpretation:
New-licence processing improved from 64.5 days in April to 47.76 days in July. PTA processing worsened from 46.48 days in March to 101.26 days in July. Renewal processing was near 10-11 days through June, then rose to 15.88 days in July. These are average-time metrics, not queue-date backlog ages.
