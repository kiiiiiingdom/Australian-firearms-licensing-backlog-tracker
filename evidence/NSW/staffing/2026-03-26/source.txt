Relevant question:
"Can you provide a current workforce snapshot of the Firearms Registry.
(a) Processing FTE
(b) Vacancies
(c) Contractors engaged
(d) Contractor expenditure for 2024-25 and 2025-26 (to date)"

Official answer:
"a) 84 permanent positions
b) 1
c) Nil
d) Nil"

Interpretation:
- The answer says "84 permanent positions"; it does not explicitly say 84 FTE or 84 filled headcount.
- Record the vacancy separately.
- No contractors were engaged at the time of the answer.
- Do not convert "permanent positions" to FTE, headcount or funded establishment without another official source.
