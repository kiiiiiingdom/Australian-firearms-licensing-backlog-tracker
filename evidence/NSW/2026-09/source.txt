Official method/caveat wording carried by this monthly report series:
- "Gun Safe data does not include paper-based applications. Paper based applications represent 2.5% of all transactions."
- Average processing times for paper-based applications are longer because of postage and manual handling.
- New-licence and PTA average processing times include the 28-day cooling-off period.

Methodological handling:
- These are monthly average-time and monthly-volume observations, not processing-date queue metrics.
- Do not calculate backlog age or speed ratio from them.
- Submitted and processed counts can relate to different cohorts; their difference must not be treated as an independently verified backlog count.
