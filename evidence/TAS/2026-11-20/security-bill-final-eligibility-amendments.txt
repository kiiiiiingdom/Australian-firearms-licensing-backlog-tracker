Scan pages 5 and 7, clause 20: partner replaces relative in proposed section 29(2)(da), while the separate household-member limb remains. Partner includes spouse or significant relationship under the Relationships Act 2003. This is a consideration in suitability assessment, not automatic rejection whenever a partner exists or a general all-relatives screening rule.

Scan page 7, clause 20 third amendment replaces proposed paragraph (h) and section 29(3)(a). It covers convictions for violence against another person, family-violence offences, and equivalent out-of-jurisdiction family-violence offences. This conviction rule is distinct from behaviour-based discretionary suitability considerations and does not impose the same ten-year period on every other refusal ground.
