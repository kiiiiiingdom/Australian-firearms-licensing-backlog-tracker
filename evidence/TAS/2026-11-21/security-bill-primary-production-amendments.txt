Scan page 22, new clause C, substitutes section 40(a). Routes are a person regularly engaged in primary production on land that person owns/manages/leases; a relative within section 29(2A) living on or near that land; an employee regularly engaged by that person; or a non-employee providing services to that person in respect of the land. This describes the replacement paragraph, not automatic approval, removal of other section 40 requirements, a definition of every relative, or a concluded comparison with all prior entitlements.

Scan pages 20-21, new clause B, proposed section 33A. The defined relevant application is Category C, has primary production as its genuine reason and falls under proposed section 40(a)(iv), the non-employee services route. Grant requires fewer than 3 associated licences already in force for relevant land below 800 hectares, or fewer than 5 for land of 800 hectares or more. These are narrowly scoped licence-grant limits, not a general firearm ownership cap or a limit automatically applied to the relative route in section 40(a)(ii). The associated authority is limited to one straight-pull action firearm, specified pest-control purposes on that relevant land and written permission from the qualifying land owner/manager/lessee; other Category C firearms and other land/purposes are excluded by the proposed provision.
