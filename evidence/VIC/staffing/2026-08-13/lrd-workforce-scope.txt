A member asked for firearms-specific full-time staff in the Licensing and Regulation Division (LRD), compared with the preceding two years.
Ministerial response: there is no dedicated group whose sole work is firearm licensing; staff also process private security licensing and prohibited-weapon approvals. The Government said Victoria Police manages detailed allocation and vacancies as operational matters.

Interpretation: firearms-specific staff totals cannot be inferred from LRD totals without a verified workload or roster allocation. Do not use division FTE as a denominator for firearms-only applications.
No numeric FTE or vacancy count was supplied by this exchange.
Evidence status: context_only; verified statement of organisational scope.
