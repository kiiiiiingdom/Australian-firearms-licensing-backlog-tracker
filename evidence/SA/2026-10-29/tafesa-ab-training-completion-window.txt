## Exact official wording
"Your training must be completed within 6 months of the issue date on your letter."

## Independent summary and scope
The TAFE SA A/B course description specifies a six-calendar-month window for completing the approved training, measured from the SAPOL approval letter's issue date. It is a completion requirement, not merely a course-registration window. The same description requires the approval letter for enrolment; the government service index links directly to TAFE SA and describes the public training system.
This record is limited to the published TAFE SA Category AB course. It does not establish a universal deadline for every training provider, licence category, club pathway or other request in a letter. No automatic extension, pause, exemption or consequence of a missed deadline is verified here. It is not a measured licensing delay, statutory minimum wait, licence term, permit validity period or staffing measure. Calendar months are preserved without converting them to a fixed day count.
