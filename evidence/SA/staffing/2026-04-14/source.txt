Source: South Australia Police, FOI Determination 26-1424
Date: 14 April 2026
URL: https://www.police.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1437406/Determination-26-1424.pdf

Relevant extract:
"Staffing levels with the FAB Registry and Adjudication sections are 17.8 substantive Full-time Equivalent (FTE) positions, however temporary staffing has increased since 2023 supplemented by a number of additional roles. Staff possess diverse skill sets and receive ongoing training. Accordingly, rosters are managed on both a daily and monthly basis as required to ensure the processing of licence applications, renewals, and permits."

The determination also states:
"There are no documents available within the scope of your request as no FAB staff are assigned to work on the National Firearms Register project."

Interpretation:
- 17.8 is substantive FTE for Registry + Adjudication.
- Temporary staffing/additional roles are acknowledged but not numerically quantified.
- Do not infer total current FTE from 17.8.
- Do not calculate backlog/FTE ratios from 17.8 while temporary staffing is unquantified.
