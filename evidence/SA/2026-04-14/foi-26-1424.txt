Methodological notes:
- The three 14 November 2025 pending counts are point-in-time queue counts.
- The 17.8 FTE figure is substantive Registry + Adjudication FTE; temporary/additional staffing is unquantified.
- Do not infer total effective FTE or calculate backlog-per-FTE ratios from 17.8 alone.
- SAPOL explicitly states that it does not set specific processing-time KPIs/targets and that legacy-system statistics have limitations.
