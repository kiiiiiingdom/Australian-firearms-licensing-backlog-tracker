Relevant official information released by SAPOL:

Applications/renewals/permits on hand:
- Firearms licence applications: 1,584; earliest received 15 November 2024
- Firearms licence renewals: 2,057; earliest received 15 December 2024
- Permits: 1,353; earliest received 3 June 2025

SAPOL stated that Firearms Branch does not record a backlog for past years; instead it tracks the number of applications currently on hand and the dates those applications were received.

On average processing times, SAPOL stated that the information exists within its computer system but is not readily retrievable in a usable format and that producing meaningful averages would require manual, case-by-case review. It cited applicant training and additional-document requirements as external factors that vary significantly.

On staffing allocations, access was refused under Clause 16(1) of Schedule 1 of the Freedom of Information Act 1991 (SA). The determination stated that disclosure could adversely affect management/assessment of personnel, effective performance of agency functions and industrial relations.

Evidence notes:
