## Original scope and date interpretation
QAO's limited-assurance follow-up of Regulating firearms (Report 8: 2020-21) disagreed with six of QPS's 13 completion claims and recommended reopening the six partial recommendations. The incomplete areas concern information integration, risk-based inspections, non-compliance follow-up, shooting-range oversight, electronic transaction notifications and interstate transfers. QAO assessed support for reported implementation and alignment with recommendations, not whether actions achieved intended outcomes. The underlying QPS self-assessments were reported between August 2023 and May 2024; neither is a newly invented exact snapshot day. The report was tabled on 5 November 2026; this is separate from retrieval, webpage update, implementation events and the undated status-as-at field.

The dated report describes a replacement-register release expectation for mid-2027. For this planning record, 5 November dates the published expectation, not delivery. It is retained separately from QPS's existing 16 October staged-release plan starting March 2027. These descriptions can cover different milestones; no live system, revised binding deadline, contradiction or proven time saving is inferred. No exact day is invented for mid-2027. Neither record measures licensing throughput, current application backlog or staffing. A cleared historical transfer-form backlog is not reclassified as a current zero application backlog.
