Exact official wording:
"From 12 March 2027:"
"Timber storage containers will no longer be approved for weapons storage."
"All Category A, B, C, E and M weapons must be stored in a solid steel safe."

Legal-status qualification:
Queensland legislation currently records Part 8 of the 2026 amendment Act as not yet proclaimed into force. The tracker therefore preserves the QPS announced 12 March 2027 compliance date while distinguishing it from provisions already commenced.

Methodological note:
This is a future storage requirement, not a licensing-processing metric. It receives no backlog or speed-ratio calculations.
