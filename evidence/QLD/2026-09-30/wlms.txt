# Queensland Police Service — Modernising Queensland's Weapons Licensing Services

Observation date: 2026-10-06
Source updated date: 2026-09-30
Source title: Modernising Queensland's Weapons Licensing Services
Source URL: https://www.police.qld.gov.au/units/weapons-licensing/modernising-queenslands-weapons-licensing-services
Evidence status: verified

Exact published wording:
Work on the replacement Weapons Licensing Management System (WLMS) has commenced, marking a major milestone in the modernisation of Queensland’s weapons licensing services.

The WLMS project will deliver a contemporary digital platform that will improve the customer experience, enhance weapons licensing service efficiency, strengthen public safety, and support Queensland’s transition to the National Firearms Register. The replacement WLMS will be delivered in staged releases planned from March 2027.
