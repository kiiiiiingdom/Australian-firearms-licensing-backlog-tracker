Official capacity reform:
QPS states that work on the replacement Weapons Licensing Management System (WLMS) has commenced. The project is intended to deliver a contemporary digital platform to improve customer experience, weapons-licensing service efficiency, public safety, and Queensland's transition to the National Firearms Register.

QPS states that staged releases are planned from March 2027.

Methodological treatment:
- Administrative/ICT capacity reform, not a staffing count.
- Do not attribute any processing-time change to the new system before deployment evidence exists.
