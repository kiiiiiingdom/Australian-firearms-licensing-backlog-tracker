Source: Western Australian Office of the Auditor General, 2025 Transparency Report - Major IT Projects, Project 9: Firearms Licensing and Registry System
URL: https://audit.wa.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Report-21_2025-Transparency-Report-Major-IT-Projects.pdf
Retrieved for tracker: 5 October 2026

Official project data:
- Original estimated total cost: $25.33 million
- Current estimated total cost: $35.12 million
- Current funding: $24.78 million
- Cost to date: $11.11 million
- Current completion date: December 2026
- Public-facing elements went live in March 2025.
- The project is intended to replace manual processes, increase automation, and digitalise WA Police firearms licensing and registry processes.

The OAG states that further funding will be required to finalise non-public-facing functionality and may also be required for national requirements.

Methodological treatment:
- Administrative/ICT capacity evidence, not staffing.
- Monetary values are retained in evidence and notes rather than converted to staffing capacity.
