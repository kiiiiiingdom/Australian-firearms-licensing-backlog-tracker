Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Verified official information:
- ACT Policing stated there were 22,739 registered firearms in the ACT and 7,118 licensed firearm holders.
- ACT Policing said its current data management system had "extremely limited search and filtering parameters for data drawdown".
- Answering the requested historical transfer question would require manual interrogation of ACT Policing systems and databases.
- The answer states the National Firearms Register and uplift of the ACT Registry are expected to improve these capabilities.
- Federal Government funding provided to date: $500,000.
- The Federal Funding Agreement is stated to contribute up to 75% ($18.7m) of the total ACT Firearms Registry uplift cost over the four-year expected life of the program.
- ACT Government contribution: up to 25% ($6.2m).
- At the time of the answer, none of the $500,000 federal funding had been allocated; ACT Government funding included a 2024-25 Budget allocation of $996,000 for design and discovery work.

Evidence status: verified

Methodological notes:
- This is Registry ICT/system-capacity and funding context, not a processing-time metric.
- Do not treat whole-program funding as staffing or FTE.
