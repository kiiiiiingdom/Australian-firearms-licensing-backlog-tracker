Relevant official capacity reform:
- ACT Government and ACT Policing are enhancing the ACT Firearms Registry.
- The Registry will transition from a paper-based system to a fully digital platform.
- Improvements include licensing and permit process changes, upgraded registry systems, new technology for NFR integration, and data-quality improvements.
