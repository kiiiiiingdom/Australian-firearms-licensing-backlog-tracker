Exact NT Police answer: "Processing timeframes are subject to the volume of applications and operational availability. For this reason, a statutory processing timeframe is not suitable."
Exact recommendation: "The Committee recommends that the Explanatory Statement be amended to explain how licence applications and renewals will be processed in a timely manner."

This finding addresses standards and renewal continuity, not a measured wait. No statutory maximum duration may be inferred from this committee report alone.
