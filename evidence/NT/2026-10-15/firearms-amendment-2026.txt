Commencement:
Northern Territory legislation records Firearms Amendment Act 2026, Act No. 10 of 2026, assent 28 May 2026 and commencement 30 June 2026.

## Licence continuation after timely renewal application

Exact official NT Police wording:
"A licence continues to be in force beyond its expiry, provided:"
- "an application for a new firearms licence of the same licence type has been lodged before the existing licence expires"
- "the application has not been granted or refused"
- "the existing licence has not been revoked."

Legislative wording sufficient to verify:
"a licence continues in force beyond its expiry if, before the expiry" the licensee has lodged an application for a new licence of the same type and the application has not been granted or refused; the continued licence expires on grant or refusal unless earlier revoked.

Interpretation:
This protects the legal continuity of a timely renewal applicant while the replacement application is pending. It is not a processing-time or backlog metric.

## Longer corporate and employee licence periods

Exact official NT Police wording:
"A new firearms corporate licence and firearms employee licence may now be issued for periods of up to 10 years."

Interpretation:
This changes maximum licence duration for those licence types. It does not quantify processing performance.

## Licence and permit conditions

Exact official NT Police wording:
"Conditions may now be added, revoked or varied during the currency of a licence or permit."

Legislative wording sufficient to verify:
The Commissioner may revoke or vary a condition of a licence or permit and may specify further conditions during the period of a licence or permit.

Interpretation:
This is a legal/administrative power affecting licence and permit conditions during their term. It is not a processing-time metric.

Methodological note:
No backlog age, processing advance, speed ratio or backlog-age-change calculations apply to these legal-policy observations.
